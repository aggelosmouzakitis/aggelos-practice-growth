The reality of private practice

Your practice has to pay the bills.

The website is live. Your directory profiles are up. You’ve rewritten your bio more times than you care to count. The gaps in your diary are still there.

Keeping another job while you build your caseload can leave you wondering when you’ll finally be able to make the transition. If referrals have slowed down, even an established practice can start to feel uncertain.

And after paying for marketing that hasn’t helped, spending more money on another expert is a hard decision.

You deserve a clear explanation of what’s happening before you invest in changing it.
